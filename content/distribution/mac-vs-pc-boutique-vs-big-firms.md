## LinkedIn post (English, 120-200 words)

Between 2006 and 2009, Apple ran 66 ads where a relaxed man said "I'm a Mac" and a stiff man in a suit said "I'm a PC."

What made it work was not an attack. The PC was competent, formal, built for business. The Mac was personal and integrated. Two different machines, for two different kinds of user.

The advisory market has the same split, and AI is making it visible.

The big firms are excellent at what they do. A multinational transforming in forty countries needs a partner that can mobilise hundreds of people in a month. That is a real strength.

But a firm with 70 people is not buying scale. They are buying the handovers: partner to manager, manager to analyst, analyst to implementation. Every handover is professional. Every handover loses a little of the why.

Different machine, different job. That is the whole story.
