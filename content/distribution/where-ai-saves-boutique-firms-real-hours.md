## LinkedIn post (English, 120-200 words)

Ask a managing partner where the firm loses time and the answer is usually "everywhere, a little."

That is honest. It is also useless, because you cannot build a system for "everywhere."

In firms of 50 to 100 people, time does not leak evenly. It pools in three flows that repeat every week: proposals, client onboarding, and documentation.

A proposal that takes six hours mostly rewrites content the firm already has. Onboarding runs through compliance checks, system entries and welcome emails by hand, every single time. Documentation lives in heads and inboxes, so the same questions get answered twice.

The fix is not a vision or a platform. It is a system built on the tools your people already open on a Tuesday, that drafts from your own material, and where a partner always approves the final version. The system prepares, the expert decides.

In the article I break down all three flows: what the friction looks like and what a realistic system looks like.
