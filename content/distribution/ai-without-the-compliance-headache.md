## LinkedIn post (English, 120-200 words)

In almost every partnership there is one person who goes quiet when AI comes up.

Often the most experienced partner in the room. Not against progress. Thinking about the client file that ends up where it should not, the regulator nobody can answer, the advice that went out with an unchecked error.

That partner is right to ask. And the questions have answers.

There is no separate privacy law for AI. GDPR applies the same way it applies to your CRM. The EU AI Act mostly sets obligations for providers, with lighter duties for firms using AI professionally. Confidentiality is a matter of tool choices and agreements, not luck. And accountability stays where it always was: with the expert whose name is on the advice.

Your people are probably already using AI. The question is whether that happens inside a framework or outside one.

The full guide covers GDPR, the AI Act, confidentiality and accountability, in practical language. Not legal advice, but a shared starting point.
